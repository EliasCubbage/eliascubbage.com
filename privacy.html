Privacy Policy

This site (eliascubbage.com) respects your privacy. Here is what you need to know:

Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site. The analytics service is loaded only after you accept our cookie consent banner.

  • We configure GA4 with anonymize_ip: true so your IP address is partially masked.
  • Advertising features (Google signals, ad personalization) are disabled.
  • No cookies are set until you click Accept on the banner.
  • You can Decline at any time to prevent tracking. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage.

What is collected

  • Page views, scroll depth, and basic interaction data
  • Browser / device type (user-agent)
  • General geographic region (country / city level, not precise)

We do not sell, share, or otherwise distribute your data to third parties beyond what is necessary for site analytics.

Your rights

  • You may clear your browser cookies/storage at any time to reset your consent preference.
  • If you have a Google account, you can manage your Google Analytics data through Google My Account.

You can also contact me via LinkedIn or the contact form on the home page.
