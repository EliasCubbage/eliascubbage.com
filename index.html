Sales Methodology

I combine proven sales frameworks with creative strategy to consistently deliver results. Here's how I approach every deal.

01

MEDDIC Qualification

I rigorously qualify opportunities using the MEDDIC framework — Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion — to ensure commercial and technological fit before investing resources.

02

SPIN Selling

Using Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-Payoff questions to uncover customer pain points, build urgency, and demonstrate value. Trained teams in this methodology, resulting in 30% pipeline growth.

03

Consultative Approach

I don't just pitch products — I diagnose problems. By understanding each prospect's unique challenges and business context, I position solutions that drive measurable outcomes and ROI.

04

ABM & Outbound

Account-based marketing combined with strategic outbound campaigns. I craft personalized messaging, email cadences, and multi-channel sequences that engage decision-makers and move deals forward.
